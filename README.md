## 👋 About Me

I'm **Arun Roshan**, an **IoT and embedded systems engineer** who builds **assistive technology** for people with visual, hearing and speech impairments.

- 🎓 **Assistant Professor (Junior)** and **full-time PhD Scholar** at the School of Computer Science Engineering and Information Systems (SCORE), **VIT Vellore**
- 🔬 **PhD research:** *An Integrated IoT–AI Framework with Context-Aware Multimodal Arbitration for Assistive Support to Individuals with Visual, Hearing, and Speech Impairments*
- 🚀 **Founder** of **[eARgle Technologies](https://eargle.tech)**, a DPIIT-recognized startup building smart assistive devices (*"Technology Empowering Ability"*)
- 🧑‍🏫 Runs **[eARgle Innovation Labs](https://earglelabs.com)**: Arduino and IoT courses and hands-on workshops for schools and colleges
- 🎥 Creator of **[Arun Roshan Tech](https://www.youtube.com/@ArunRoshanTechOfficial)** on YouTube (tech, electronics and coding) and a Radio Jockey at VIT Community Radio
- 🌐 Full portfolio: **[arunroshan.eargle.tech](https://arunroshan.eargle.tech)**
- 💬 Ask me about **ESP32, Arduino, sensor fusion, edge AI, BLE, Firebase and Flutter apps**
